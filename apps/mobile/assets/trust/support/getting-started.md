# Getting started with MeMy

MeMy is a personal life OS for goals, money, habits, calendar, and optional health summaries.

## First steps

1. Sign in (demo auth may be used in this build).
2. Open **Today** for your daily overview.
3. Use **Plan** or the side menu to open Goals, Finance, Habits, Calendar, or Health.
4. Visit **Settings → Privacy** to see what stays on your device.

## Tips

- Connected Apps manages Calendar and Health connections.
- Help & Support has short articles for each area.
- Features labeled Planned are intentionally not interactive yet.
