# Financial Disclaimer

**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-10  
**Product:** MeMy by MoonTech

MeMy Finance lets you manually log income and expenses for personal budgeting awareness.

## Not financial advice

MeMy does **not** provide investment, tax, accounting, or professional financial advice. Figures you enter are for your own tracking.

## Data handling (this build)

- Transactions and categories are stored on this device only.
- Finance data is not synced to a MeMy backend in this build.
- Finance data is not sent to AI providers in this build.
- You can export or delete local finance data from Privacy & Data.

## Accuracy

Currency formatting and summaries depend on the amounts and categories you enter. MeMy does not connect to banks or payment processors in this build.
