# Building Habits

Habits track recurring routines with check-ins and streak-style progress.

## Storage

Habits and check-ins are stored on this device only. They are not sent to a MeMy backend or AI in this build.

## Tips

- Choose a schedule that matches how you actually live the habit.
- Check in from the habit detail screen.
- Wipe habits from Privacy & Data if you want a clean slate (does not reseed demo data).
