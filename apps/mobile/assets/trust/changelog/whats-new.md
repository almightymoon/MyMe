# What's new

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-10

- MeMy is your Personal Life Companion: everything you record stays on this device. No account, no cloud sync, no live AI.
- New first-run setup: choose your base currency, units, week start, and time zone, then optionally connect your calendar and Health. Nothing is read until you grant permission.
- Fresh installs start empty — no sample Goals, transactions, or Habits pretending to be yours.
- Signed-out demo screens are gone — MeMy opens straight into your day.
- Menus now show only what actually works. Coach, Wardrobe, Body, meal logging, and notification reminders are not part of this release.
- Exercise items open real detail with safety notes instead of a dismissive message.
- Trust foundation: Privacy & Data Center, Help & Support, Legal drafts, About
- Local export and wipe for Goals, Finance, Habits, and MeMy calendar cache
- Deleting your data also resets setup, so the app genuinely returns to a first launch
- Health connection summary export only (no raw samples)
- Accurate data catalog reflecting on-device vs platform stores
