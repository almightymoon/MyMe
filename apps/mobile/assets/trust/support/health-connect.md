# Connecting Health

MeMy can read selected wellness metrics from HealthKit (iOS) or Health Connect (Android).

## Important

- Read-only — MeMy never writes to platform Health.
- Not medical advice — see the Health disclaimer.
- Health values are not sent to AI or MeMy API in this build.

## Steps

1. Open Health → Connect.
2. Choose the metric groups you want MeMy to request.
3. Grant permission in the system sheet.
4. Return to MeMy to see daily summaries.

Disconnect clears MeMy’s connection prefs and in-memory summaries only.
