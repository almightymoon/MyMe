Privacy Policy

Draft — not legally reviewed.

MeMy uses Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple. The backend stores the provider plus provider subject, optional email, profile, preferences, and synchronized app-owned records in PostgreSQL on the operator's VPS. Wardrobe images use private object storage. Raw HealthKit / Health Connect records and imported device calendar events are not uploaded.

On your device

  • Health platform data (steps, sleep, workouts, etc.)
  • Device calendar events you choose to connect

In the cloud

  • Account identity and preferences
  • Synced goals, habits, finance, and wardrobe metadata
  • Private wardrobe images (not public URLs)
