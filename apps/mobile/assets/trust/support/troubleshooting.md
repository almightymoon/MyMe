# Troubleshooting

## App looks empty after wipe

Deleting local data clears MeMy’s store. Demo seed data is not re-created after the first launch flag is set.

## Calendar won’t sync

Confirm calendar permission in system Settings, then reopen Connected Apps → Calendar. Try disconnect and reconnect.

## Health shows no numbers

Check that the metric group is granted. On iOS, HealthKit may not reveal which read categories were approved — MeMy explains this uncertainty in the UI.

## Need help

Use Help & Support → Report a problem. Reports include allowlisted diagnostics only (no event titles or health values).
