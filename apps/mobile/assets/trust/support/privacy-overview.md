# Privacy & your data

Open **Settings → Privacy** (Privacy & Data Center) for a module-by-module catalog.

## Highlights

- Finance and Habits are on-device only in this build.
- Goals may use the MeMy API only when built with `GOALS_DATA_SOURCE=api`.
- Health samples stay on the platform; MeMy keeps config + in-memory summaries.
- Diagnostics reports are allowlisted operational fields only.

Use Export and Delete carefully — wipes do not remove HealthKit / Health Connect data or external calendar events.
